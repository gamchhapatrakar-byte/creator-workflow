# सपनों का घर — lyric film, Swiss Motion Graphics

87.2 s · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 24 fps · audio: the user's song (Suno), unchanged · lyrics burned in, line by line, word by word.

## Three structures

1. **The house that hard work builds** (chosen). One grid of blocks is built into a house during the mukhda, one block per beat of effort; the paper leak removes the one red block (the exam paper / the dream) and the house collapses; in the antara the same blocks are re-flowed into what took them: a chair (कुर्सी), a ledger sheet (हिसाब), then sold off one by one (बाज़ार). The red block comes back alone at the end.
2. **OMR answer sheet.** A grid of bubbles that a student fills in; the leak empties it. Strong idea, but bubbles become a chart; less emotional turn.
3. **Countdown to the exam.** A giant day counter falling to zero. Too much like a timer, misses "घर".

Chosen 1: one subject (the house of dreams), one turn (the leak), and the grid can mean something (each block = one drop of मेहनत).

## Logline and arc

A student's dream, built block by block on a grid, is taken by one leaked paper and resold as a politician's chair.
Setup (0–27 s): dot → grid → house. Turn (27–40 s): the red block slips off the grid, the house drops. Ending (43–87 s): chair → ledger → sold → one red block alone on an empty grid, the title flush left.

## Benchmark

Zurich-school poster grids and modern identity-in-motion systems (grammar only): learn flush-left type over large white space, snap-on-the-beat, extreme scale contrast. Don't take any composition, typeface or mark.

## System

- Page `#EAE9E5`, ink `#111111`, greys `#C9C7C1` / `#DAD8D2`, **one signal colour: red `#E30613`** = the dream / exam paper. Only that block is ever red.
- Type: Hind (OFL, Indian Type Foundry), one family, Medium/SemiBold/Bold. Devanagari only on screen (film language Hindi).
- Grid: 6 columns, 72 px margins, 24 px gutters. Upper zone = programme (section label, line counter, the lyric); lower zone = artifact (block grid, 8 × 6 modules of 108 px).
- Tempo from the song: ≈139.5 BPM, beat 0.430 s, phase 0.202 s. Every block snap lands on a beat.
